# MVP Template

**Core problem:**
People have more free time than they realize, but often struggle with boredom, loneliness, or finding meaningful ways to spend it. Meanwhile, people in their communities need help with everyday tasks.

**Must-have features:**
- Account creation and login/logout.
- Ability to create requests for help.
- Ability to browse and accept requests.
- Basic messaging between matched users.
- Built-in buddy system and reporting features to discourage harassment.

**Nice to have, but not MVP:**
- Clean, round, bubbly UI.
- Swipe-based animations.
- User ratings and achievements.
- Notifications.

**Out of scope for now:**
- Payments or financial transactions.
- Large-scale volunteering events.
- AI recommendations.
- Advanced social features.

**Key assumptions to test:**
- People are willing to help strangers with small tasks.
- People feel comfortable requesting help.
- A simple, swipe-based system encourages participation.
- Helping others improves users' sense of connection and purpose.
- Basic safety features make users comfortable meeting others.
