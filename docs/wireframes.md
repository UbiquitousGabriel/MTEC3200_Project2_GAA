<details>
<summary>Main action per screen (hidden for testing — don't show participants)</summary>

1. **Landing:** Tap "Get started" to create an account
2. **Sign in / Create account:** Sign in (or create an account)
3. **Map of requests:** Tap a request circle, then "I can help"
4. **Browse requests (swipe):** Swipe right ("Help") to offer help on a request
5. **Ask for help:** Fill in the request and tap "Post request"
6. **Messages:** Open a conversation with a match
7. **Chat + safety:** Send a message to plan the meet-up (Report and Bring a buddy are always here)
8. **Profile:** Turn "Available to help" on or off

</details>
