# Recruiting Script

## Outreach message (3 sentences: who I am, what I'm researching, time commitment)

> Hi, my name is Gabriel, and I'm a student at City Tech working on a design research project. I'm learning about how people in a community ask for help with everyday things, and how people who'd be willing to help find out who needs it, and I'd love to hear about your experience. The conversation takes about 15 minutes, in person or by phone/video, and participation is completely optional.

**Check — the message does not:**
- [x] Promote a product
- [x] Name a solution (no "app," "platform," or "tool")
- [x] Apply pressure ("completely optional," no deadline)

## Consent statement (read aloud before recording)

> This interview is for a class project at City Tech. I'd like to record it so I can take accurate notes. You can skip any question or stop at any time, and your full name won't be used — you'll be referred to by a code like "P1." Do I have your permission to record?

If they decline recording, take written notes instead. Write **"Consent given"** and the date at the top of the interview file.

## Where to send it (outside my circle)
- Neighbors / building group chat or bulletin board
- Staff or regulars at local businesses (laundromat, bodega, café)
- Members of a community org, mutual-aid group, or library program I'm not personally close to
- Online communities: neighborhood subreddit, Facebook "Buy Nothing" / mutual-aid groups, Discord servers
- Coworkers I don't know well
- Friends-of-friends I haven't met

**Goal:** send to at least 3 people today. Need 5 completed interviews (solo).

## Safety
Meet in public or by video. Tell someone when meeting in person. No minors. Don't share home address. End the interview if uncomfortable.
